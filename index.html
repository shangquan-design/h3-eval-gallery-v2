Hidden-state attribute vectors
Per-block (L30-L49) steering. Quality is a clean dial; gravity/camera/brightness mostly don't work at any safe magnitude.
Velocity-space attribute vectors
Same method, steering the consolidated noise_pred instead of per-block hidden states (Michael's suggestion). Quality works even better (clean at full magnitude); camera's "motion" signal turns out to be a color-flicker artifact, not real panning.
